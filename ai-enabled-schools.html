What it takes

Six things a school must show, every year.

We verify each one from the learning platform and from our own campus records — not from a form the school fills in.

Every student, not a club

Every student in the participating grades is learning on the gamified AI curriculum, in a protected weekly period.

Mastery, tracked child by child

Progress is measured by topics mastered at 80% or more and mistakes fixed — not by attendance or completion.

Teachers trained

The teachers who run the AI period have completed our hands-on teacher workshops, and keep attending each term.

Engineers on campus

An industry engineer has spent a day on campus every month, clearing doubts and running a live build sprint.

Students build and present

Every student completes a grade-specific capstone, and the school has held its annual Build-a-thon.

Responsible and safe

No student uses a personal AI account, consent and safeguarding policies are in place, and responsible AI is taught in every grade.

What your school gets

A mark you can show everywhere parents look.

  • The badge kit. The AI-Enabled seal and verified tick, sized for your website, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and admissions brochures, with the academic year on it.
  • A plaque and certificate. For your reception and your principal's office, naming the grades covered.
  • A public listing. Your school on the register on this page, so anyone can check the badge is genuine.
  • A parent summary. A one-page report of what your students learnt, built and presented that year — ready for the newsletter.
  • An announcement moment. We present the badge at your Build-a-thon awards ceremony, in front of students and parents.
  • Renewed every year. The badge carries the academic year, so it stays meaningful. If the program lapses, the listing comes down.
